# Executive-Retail-Sales-Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

The Executive Retail Sales Analysis Dashboard provides an interactive and visual summary of retail performance across multiple dimensions including region, category, city, and time.
It helps executives monitor key sales metrics, identify top-performing regions, and analyze profit trends to support data-driven decisions.
This project is built using Tableau and based on the popular Superstore Dataset.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze Year-to-Date (YTD) Sales, Profit, Quantity, and Discount.

- Compare Sales vs. Goals across different regions.

- Visualize Sales and Profit by City on a geographical map.

- Track Sales and Profit by Month to identify seasonal trends.

- Evaluate Category and Sub-Category performance to spot key growth areas.

## Dashboard Features
1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Displayed on the left panel:

💰 YTD Sales : $1343k

📈 YTD Profit : $175k

📦 YTD Quantity : 22k

🎯 Average Discount : 15.57%

2. Sales vs Goals by Region

Highlights how each region (West, East, Central, South) is performing against set goals.
Green indicates regions achieving or exceeding goals; red shows underperformance.

3. Sales and Profit by City

A dynamic map view showing cities by size (sales volume) and color (profitability).
Enables regional and city-level performance insights.

4. Sales and Profit by Category and Sub-Category

Treemap visual representing performance by product category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology) and sub-categories like Chairs, Tables, Phones, etc.
Green = Profit, Red = Loss.

5. Sales vs Profit by Month

Combination of bar and line chart for sales and profit trends over time.
Shows seasonality and identifies high-performing months.

## 🧠 Insights Summary

Central Region achieved its target successfully.

New York City was the top-performing city in terms of profit.

East and West Regions underperformed in terms of sales goals.

Technology category (especially Copiers and Phones) drove the highest profit.

Furniture (Tables) showed consistent losses.

Strong year-end sales peaks suggest holiday seasonality effect.

## 🗂️ Dataset Information

Dataset: Sample Superstore
Fields Used:

Region, Category, Sub-Category, City

Sales, Profit, Quantity, Discount

Order Date, Ship Mode
